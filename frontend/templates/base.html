This check is not exhaustive, and a good grade is not a certificate. It reads what a publicly reachable OpenCloud instance shows an anonymous visitor: its version, the advisories against that version, its transport, its headers and a set of settings that are visible without logging in. An “A” means none of those went wrong - not that the instance is secure. Everything behind the login, the server it runs on, the network around it, the data in it and the people with accounts on it are outside what any unauthenticated scan can see. Treat the result as one input among several, never as a security audit or a penetration test.
