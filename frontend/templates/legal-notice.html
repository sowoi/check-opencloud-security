Understanding the necessity of an Impressum under German law

In adherence to German law, providing an "Impressum" (legal notice) is a mandatory component for websites operated from within Germany or directed towards a German audience. This legal stipulation, embedded within the Telemedia Act (Telemediengesetz - TMG) and the State Media Treaties (Rundfunkstaatsvertrag - RStV), is designed to uphold transparency and accountability by mandating websites to contain a clear and accessible statement with information about the website owner or publisher, including contact details and, if applicable, additional regulatory information.
